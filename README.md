SandnSea – Real Estate Portfolio Website

A modern and elegant portfolio website created for Sand N Sea Realty, Mumbai, designed to highlight their premium real estate offerings. The platform serves as a digital showcase of the diverse properties they deal in – from luxurious residences to commercial spaces – presented with a clean, user-friendly interface.

The website not only reflects the brand’s professionalism but also provides potential clients with an engaging way to explore property listings, discover details, and connect with Sand N Sea Realty for their real estate needs.
## Tech Stack

**Client:** React, TailwindCSS
